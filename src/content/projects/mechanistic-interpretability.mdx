## Method

At a high level, patching measures the causal effect of a component's activation on a target
metric. For a logit difference metric $\mathcal{M}$, the patched effect of replacing a clean
activation $a$ with a corrupted activation $a'$ is:

$$
\Delta \mathcal{M} = \mathcal{M}\big(\text{run with } a' \big) - \mathcal{M}\big(\text{run with } a\big)
$$
